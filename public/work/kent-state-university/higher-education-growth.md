## A clearer path to graduate study.

At Fyntix, we brought search visibility, paid campaigns, content and conversion design together for Kent State University’s graduate business programmes.

Graduate study is a considered decision. Prospective students need to understand programme fit, compare their options and find a useful next step. Search creates an opportunity to answer those questions; the experience after the click gives that opportunity a direction.

Our focus connected discovery with enquiries, bringing the message, the landing page and the conversion experience into the same growth programme.

## What we delivered.

### Programme intent

We aligned search targeting with prospective graduate students. A useful programme journey begins with the decision someone is trying to make, so visibility and relevance need to work together.

### Search and content

We refined keywords on priority pages and strengthened audience-focused content. Clear explanations help a visitor connect a search question with a relevant programme and an understandable next step.

### Paid search journeys

We refined PPC targeting and landing pages. The search ad and the page it opens should support the same student decision, giving a campaign a coherent route from interest to enquiry.

### Conversion design

We created custom page templates in Drupal to support conversions. Page structure, useful information and clear enquiry routes help people act when they are ready to discuss their options.

## Read each result in context.

The figures cover different years and measures. Traffic comparisons describe visits; lead comparisons describe enquiries. Enrolments represent a later step in the student journey. Review each alongside its reporting period.

The overall-lead chart uses an index: 2016 is set to 100 and 2017 to 235. This expresses the reported percentage increase. Each year-over-year comparison has its own baseline.

“10+” and “nearly 3×” retain their reported qualifiers. Raw counts, campaign budgets, the ROI calculation and attribution details were not supplied with these results.
