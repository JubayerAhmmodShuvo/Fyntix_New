# Fyntix vs Faculty AI

Two very different ways to buy AI delivery. Every fact below is sourced and dated, including the ones that do not flatter us.

Independent

- Own platform: Agent Mesh, owned by the client

## Faculty AI

Part of Accenture

AI strategy, design, infrastructure, development, operations and change, plus its own Frontier platform.

- Size: 400+ AI native professionals joined Accenture
- Founded: 2014
- Owner: Accenture, since 16 March 2026
- Own platform: Frontier, a decision intelligence platform

Built with teams at: Google, YouTube, Revolut, Tesco, dataiku, Sage, choreograph, SMARTLY.IO

## Faculty is now Accenture's AI arm. Fyntix is a small independent team that builds inside yours.

Faculty has been part of Accenture since 16 March 2026. It offers AI strategy, design, infrastructure, development, operations and change, builds its own Frontier platform, and suits large, high-stakes AI programmes, especially in government, defence and health.

Fyntix suits product and tech teams who want AI products built with embedded engineers, product and design. Which fits better depends on the size of the programme and who you want working inside your team.

## Where each one is stronger

### Fyntix

- Product and design in the same team: Our AI Product Squads put designers, engineers and product managers in one embedded team, to shape what to build from the outcome you need and ship it to production.
- A team that grows with the scope: Engagements start with forward-deployed engineers against a scoped workflow and expand into a full AI Product Squad once the scope proves out.
- Agent Mesh, deployed as a service: A connected agent framework our AI engineers deploy and run inside your business. Your knowledge, your workflows, your IP to keep.
- You own what we build: Portable across model providers and extendable by your own team, so the capability transfers in-house over time.

### Faculty AI

- Government, defence and health: Faculty built the NHS Covid Early Warning System, and lists public services, defence, national security and law enforcement, and health and care among the sectors it serves. Fyntix has no public sector work.
- Specialist AI safety work: AI Safety and AI Due Diligence are specialist services at Faculty, alongside its six core AI services. Fyntix does not offer either as a separate service.
- Its own platform: Faculty builds Frontier, which it calls a Decision Intelligence platform, and Accenture says Frontier joins its product suite. Fyntix builds on the stack that suits each client and does not sell a platform.
- Accenture behind it: Faculty has been part of Accenture since 16 March 2026, and more than 400 AI native professionals joined Accenture with it. For a buyer who wants an AI team inside a global firm, that is what Faculty now offers.
- Training at scale: Faculty's Fellowship programme says it has trained more than 500 Fellows.

## When to choose each

### Choose Fyntix if

- You're a product or tech team that wants an AI product built and shipped, with engineering, product and design in one embedded team.
- You have a scoped workflow and want senior engineers in your team to automate it, with the capability passing to your people as it ships.
- You want to test the idea with an AI Launchpad workshop or a proof of concept before a larger build.
- You want to own what we build: your roadmap, your IP and your system.

### Choose Faculty AI if

- You run a large AI programme in government, defence, national security or health.
- You want specialist AI safety or AI due diligence work.
- You want a decision intelligence platform such as Frontier as part of the work.
- You already buy from Accenture and want AI delivery through the same firm.

### When we're not the right fit

- A regulated, multi-country programme that needs a global firm.
- Public sector work: we have not done any.

### How does Fyntix compare to Faculty AI?

Faculty is now Accenture's AI arm, offering AI strategy, design, infrastructure, development, operations and change, and its own Frontier platform. ### Fyntix vs Faculty AI: which should we choose?

Choose Fyntix if you want an AI product built and shipped with an embedded squad that you own. Choose Faculty if you run a large programme in government, defence, national security or health, want specialist AI safety work, or want delivery through Accenture. The decision section above lists the cases for each.

### Should we hire an embedded AI delivery team like Fyntix or a big consultancy?

An embedded team like Fyntix puts engineering, product and design inside your team and hands the capability to your people as it ships. A large consultancy suits a regulated, multi-country programme that needs a global firm. We set out where each fits in the decision section, including where we are not the right fit.

### Is Fyntix a good agency for AI consulting?

Fyntix is an AI consultancy and product development team. Our AI Product Squads put designers, engineers and product managers in one embedded team, and we deploy Agent Mesh, a connected agent framework your business owns. Our work for Google Cloud, Tesco and Upstix is in the featured work above.

### Who is Faculty AI best for?

Faculty suits large, high-stakes AI programmes, especially in government, defence and health. It lists public services, defence, national security and law enforcement, and health and care among the sectors it serves, offers specialist AI safety and AI due diligence, and builds its own Frontier platform.

### Is Faculty still independent?

No. Faculty has been part of Accenture since 16 March 2026, and more than 400 AI native professionals joined Accenture with it. Fyntix is independent.
