# Cookie Policy

Updated 24 November 2026

This policy covers cookies and similar storage added by Fyntix to this website. Our cookie settings let you choose whether optional external media can load. You can browse the rest of the site without allowing it.

## What this website uses

Fyntix currently sets one necessary preference cookie and does not install analytics tags, Google Ads conversion tags, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel or advertising remarketing tags on this website. Providing SEO and advertising services to clients does not mean those tools are active here.

| Item | Purpose | Duration | Your control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fyntix_cookie_preferences | Remembers the version of your privacy choice, whether external media is allowed and when the choice was saved. Contains no name or contact details. | 180 days from your latest saved choice. | Always active after a choice; clear it using your browser to reset the prompt. |
| Vimeo external media | A video player receives technical information such as an IP address when loaded and may set its own cookies or use browser storage. | Set by Vimeo under its current policies. | Optional; blocked until you allow external media. |

Any storage required by the hosting or security infrastructure is controlled by those providers, rather than optional analytics or advertising tags added by Fyntix.

## Choose or change your preferences

On your first visit, choose “Reject optional”, “Customise” or “Allow videos”. The settings panel shows necessary preferences separately from optional Vimeo media. Optional media is off until you choose to allow it.

Use “Cookie settings” in the footer or the control below whenever you want to review, save or withdraw your choice. Rejecting optional media prevents new Vimeo players from loading and removes an open player. Closing the settings panel without saving leaves your previous choice unchanged.

[Cookie settings](https://fyntix.example/cookie-policy/)

If your browser blocks the preference cookie, your saved choice applies for the current visit and the site will explain that it could not persist it. Clearing cookies, using a different browser or reaching the 180-day expiry can cause the prompt to appear again. An old preference from the imported website is not treated as permission for Vimeo.

## External services and browser controls

Once you allow and load Vimeo, that provider’s own [Privacy Policy](https://vimeo.com/privacy) applies to its processing. Withdrawing permission here stops the embedded player; it cannot recall information already sent or directly delete cookies belonging to Vimeo’s domain. Use your browser’s site-data controls to remove those cookies or storage.

External links, including maps and social sites, open services outside this website. Their cookies and settings are managed by those providers. Browser settings can also block or delete cookies, although removing necessary storage can reset preferences or affect site functionality.

## Changes and questions

If we add analytics, advertising tags or another optional provider, we will update this notice and the choice controls before activating that optional processing. For questions about this policy or personal information, email [info@fyntix.com](mailto:info@fyntix.com) or read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).
