## About Fyntix

Fyntix is an AI product design and development studio helping ambitious businesses turn AI into products, workflows and systems that deliver real-world value.

The company designs and builds AI agents, AI workflows and AI-native products that businesses own, connecting AI to the knowledge, tools and processes that make each organisation unique.

Working with global brands and high-growth businesses including Google, Tesco, Revolut, Sage and Upstix, Fyntix combines strategy, product, design and engineering in small, senior teams that move from idea to production quickly.

Its work spans enterprise agent systems, AI-powered customer products, workflow automation and AI-native software, from an agent system used by 5,000 Google Cloud sellers to an AI creative studio that reduced Tesco's retail-media approval cycle from four weeks to days.

Fyntix is headquartered in London.

## Leadership

### Jordan Richards, CEO & Co-Founder, Fyntix

Jordan Richards is CEO and Co-Founder of Fyntix.

Jordan began his career at Google at 18 through an apprenticeship, going on to become a design lead and work on global-scale design, content and technology solutions. That experience shaped his belief that technology is most valuable when it is grounded in real user needs and built to work at scale.

He went on to build and lead businesses across design, technology and creative production before bringing those disciplines together at Fyntix. Today, he works with leadership teams to identify where AI can create genuine business value and turn those opportunities into working products and systems.

Under Jordan's leadership, Fyntix has worked with organisations including Google, Tesco, Revolut, Sage and Upstix. He regularly speaks and writes about AI adoption, AI investment and value, building versus buying AI, the future of work, AI native products, entrepreneurship and what it takes to move from AI pilots to systems that businesses can actually rely on.

Media topics: AI adoption and value; AI investment; AI strategy; Build vs buy; AI native businesses; Entrepreneurship; Design and technology; Apprenticeships and alternative routes into tech; Leadership and building high-performing teams

[Download headshot](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/headshots/andabove-jordan-richards.jpg)

### Josh King, CTO & Co-Founder, Fyntix

Josh King is CTO and Co-Founder of Fyntix, where he leads the engineering and technical development of AI products, agent systems and enterprise workflows.

With a background spanning Google, Tesla and Vodafone, Josh has spent his career working at the intersection of technology, product and implementation. His focus is on turning complex technical possibilities into systems that work reliably in real business environments.

At Fyntix, Josh leads the technical teams responsible for taking AI systems from concept and prototype through to production. His work covers agent architecture, AI product development, enterprise integrations, evaluation and the engineering infrastructure required to make AI systems dependable at scale.

Josh is particularly interested in the gap between what AI models can do in isolation and what businesses need from production systems: reliable outputs, access to the right context and knowledge, integration with existing tools, measurable performance and appropriate human oversight.

Media topics: AI engineering; Agentic systems; AI architecture; Building AI in-house; AI quality and evaluation; Enterprise AI implementation; AI infrastructure; AI product development; Productionising AI; The technical realities behind AI adoption

## Logos

The Fyntix logo has two marks: the wordmark and the icon. Use them only in the approved colour pairs below. Each file is available as SVG and PNG.

### Wordmark

The primary mark. Use it by default in external communications, especially where people are still getting to know the brand.

- Chalk on Slate: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-chalk.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-chalk.png)
- Spark on Slate: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-spark.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-spark.png)
- Slate on Haze: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-slate.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-slate.png)
- Slate on Spark: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-slate.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-slate.png)
- White on Black: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-white.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-white.png)
- Black on White: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-black.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/wordmark/andabove-wordmark-black.png)

### Icon

The secondary mark, for small spaces such as favicons, social avatars and app tiles. Use the circle by default and the square only when the medium needs it.

- Circle, Slate on Spark: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-circle-spark.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-circle-spark.png)
- Circle, Chalk on Slate: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-circle-slate.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-circle-slate.png)
- Square, Slate on Spark: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-square-spark.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-square-spark.png)
- Square, Chalk on Slate: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-square-slate.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-square-slate.png)
- Glyph, Slate: [SVG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-slate.svg), [PNG](https://fyntix.example/brand-assets/logos/icon/andabove-icon-slate.png)

- **Naming:** Write the name as "Fyntix".
- **Colour:** Use the logo only in the approved pairs above. Use black or white only when contrast or a technical limit requires it.
- **Clear space:** One third of the logo height on every side.
- **Minimum size:** Wordmark 20 px tall on screen, 6 mm tall in print.
