# Webinar Recording

Build vs buy: the AI decision every leader is making right now.

For leaders bringing AI to their team and its workflows: operations, sales, marketing, finance, HR and beyond. If you're the one leading the way in your discipline, this hour is for you. Give us your details and we'll email you the recording. We covered how to bring AI to your team's workflows in-house, do it well, and skip the burden: evals, maturity levels and real build examples.

Almost every team has moved past experimentation. People are building their own prompts, workflows and agents, and getting real value from them. But step back and the picture is different: the same task automated five times in five different chat windows, knowledge scattered across hubs and channels, no way to verify which version is correct, and duplicated spend on both human hours and tokens.

So the question lands on your desk: build vs buy? We showed why it's not that binary.

## What we covered

In this 60 minute executive discussion, we explored:

- Why the instinct to build in-house is right, and why DIY builds stall half-finished.
- The four-level AI maturity model, and where your organisation actually sits on it.
- Why individual AI wins don't add up to a company system, and what that costs in duplicated hours and token spend.
- What separates a prototype from a production system: evals, confidence scores and continuous optimisation, explained properly.
- Inside real builds: Tesco's creative compliance, Google Cloud's agentic sales system and Sage's creative intelligence agent.
- The third option most leaders haven't priced: build in-house without the in-house burden.
- How to decide, workflow by workflow, whether to build, buy or embed.

Everyone who watches also gets free access to our AI Workflow Readiness Score, and where it's the right fit, an invitation to a Workflow Mapping Session: we sit with you, map five workflows in your business, and give you an honest build, buy or embed verdict on each.
