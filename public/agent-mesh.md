# The connected agent framework you own

Agent Mesh is a framework for connected agents, deployed by Fyntix's AI engineers. Your knowledge, your workflows, your IP to keep.

[SEE WHAT'S INCLUDED](#section-agent-mesh-layers)

![Agent Mesh answering a Q2 sales question in Slack with a revenue table and an anomaly flag](https://fyntix.example/content-media/pages/agent-mesh/mesh-slack-q2-sales.gen.webp)

THE PROBLEM

## A human & agent team is vital to compete

but, we've seen where businesses get stuck

### 01 Everyone builds their own

Everyone gets a licence and training, but people build their own individual workflows. The duplication is expensive for the CFO and chaos for the COO.

Cost: Duplicated effort

### 02 Knowledge is lost

Shared wikis, hubs and skill libraries, nobody uses them or keeps them up to date, so AI still starts from a blank slate.

Cost: Low quality work

### 03 Stuck in frontier models

Lots of AI usage and agents means a huge AI bill. Most tokens are spent on frontier models doing basic routine work.

Cost: Expensive token bill

### 04 Built to 75%

Teams build their own systems to 75%, workflows are part-built, with no measurement, no owner improving them and no governance.

Cost: Stalled workflows


THE PRODUCT

## That's why we built Agent Mesh:

a framework for connected agents, deployed by our AI experts, where you own your IP.

### 01 System layer: Agents built for your business

We design agents and workflows with shared company skills, tool integrations and permissioned sub-agents, so individual wins become company capability.

#### Shared group and company workflows

Company systems are in one admin layer and auto-deployed, so nobody has to search a shared hub.

#### Promote individual wins to company capability

The best skill one person built becomes the team standard rather than sitting in one person's chat history.

#### Continuous optimisation

Skills improve based on evals, confidence scores and usage, and every personal agent gets the best version.

#### Where your team already works

Personal agents live in Slack or your chat app of choice, so nobody has to learn a new tool.

![The Agent Mesh routines admin, showing scheduled workflows assigned to groups](https://fyntix.example/content-media/pages/agent-mesh/mesh-routines-admin.gen.webp)

### 02 Context layer: Unified company knowledge

Every agent starts from what your business already knows, with answers linked to verified sources. No more starting from scratch.

#### Ground work in what the business knows

Company knowledge lives in people's heads, disparate hubs and disconnected tools. Mesh captures decisions and client context, then uses them to power the work.

#### Verified sources for confidence

Anyone can use a frontier model trained on the internet. Answers in Mesh are grounded in your business, with every claim linked to a memory or tool source.

#### A wiki that builds itself

Interactions build context across the company. We use this to auto-generate your company wiki and keep it up to date.

#### Integrate your tool stack

Connect tools with native and MCP integrations. Bring your own stack, and each agent inherits its user's permissions.

![Agent Mesh gathering context from Gmail and its own memory before answering a client question](https://fyntix.example/content-media/pages/agent-mesh/mesh-gathering-context.gen.webp)

### 03 Quality layer: Outcomes you can rely on

The pipework required to scale a system you can trust, meaning routing, guardrails, evals and measurement.

#### Guardrails and human-in-the-loop

Encode your policies. High-risk actions such as contract approvals trigger the right level of human review.

#### Evals and observability

Confidence measured for every agent and skill. Know what is working, catch what is not.

#### Stop paying frontier prices for routine work

Mesh routes each request to the right model for the job, across providers and without losing context. Bring your own keys.

Watches client buying signals and recommends upsell opportunities by stage.
