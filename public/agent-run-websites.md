## Questions

### Is this GEO?

GEO is part of it: making sure AI assistants find and recommend you. We go further, building a site agents can act on and connecting it to your sales calls.

### What is AX?

AX, agent experience, is how easily an AI assistant can understand your site and act on it for a buyer, such as getting a quote or booking a call. We design for it alongside UX, so the site works for both.

### Do we lose control?

No. Nothing publishes without approval, and you own everything we build.

### Can you keep our current design?

Yes. We rebuild your site exactly as it is, or redesign it. Either way it's custom code built for agents, and your team can still change things by hand.

### How do we make changes?

Two ways. Edit pages yourself, or message the website agent in Slack or Teams: it builds the change from your design system, sends you a preview and publishes when you approve.

### Do we own the site?

Yes. You own the code, the design system and the content from day one. If you only want the rebuild, there's nothing more to pay us.

### What does the sales engine do each month?

Agents work every stage of the sale: getting you recommended by AI assistants, finding and contacting companies that fit, researching every lead, learning from your sales calls, drafting proposals and chasing follow-ups. We tune each part every month against meetings booked and pipeline.

### Can we start with one part?

Yes. Start with the website, then add the parts that matter most to you, such as outreach or call intelligence. Most clients build up to the full engine.

### Who do the agents talk to, and as whom?

Your prospects, in your name and your voice. You approve the messaging before anything goes out, and every reply lands with your team.

### Does it work with our CRM and tools?

Yes. It connects to the tools you already use, including your CRM, calendar and Slack or Teams, so nothing lives in a separate system.

### How long is the commitment?

The sales engine runs on a 12-month term, with the website rebuild included. We review the numbers with you every month.
