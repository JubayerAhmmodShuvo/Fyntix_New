SOLUTION

## Build an AI engine your business owns.

We connect AI to your key workflows, knowledge and systems. It becomes part of how your business operates, rather than another licence your teams have to manage.

### Estate & letting agents Win more instructions & convert more leads.

Reply to portal leads in minutes, book viewings and valuations without the back and forth, and draft listings and offer letters in one click. Your negotiators spend the day with people, not paperwork.

### Property management & BTR Increase the number of homes you can manage.

Answer resident questions and log repairs the moment they come in. Chase arrears, renewals and compliance dates automatically. Give your team back the hours lost to admin and re-entering in systems.

### Builders & developers Take in leads & quote faster, keep sites moving.

Respond to every enquiry faster and know which ones are worth chasing. Turn a spec into a quote in hours, not days. Keep materials ordered on time and spot the delays before they cost you a build week.

### Proptech Build AI into your product to scale revenue.

Build AI with your own data, integrated with how users already work. Scale revenue without needing to increase headcount. We design and ship it with your team, and you own what gets built.
