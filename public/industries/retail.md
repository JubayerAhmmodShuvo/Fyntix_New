RETAIL SERVICES

# Build AI into the way retail works.

Retail businesses have no shortage of systems, data or AI tools. The opportunity is using them to increase sales, improve customer loyalty and automate the work that slows teams down. We design and build AI agents, workflows and products that help your business operate more efficiently and create new opportunities for growth.

SOLUTION

## Turn AI into measurable business impact.

We build AI into the workflows that already run your business, helping teams sell more, serve customers faster, reduce manual work and make better commercial decisions.

### Increase customer loyalty

Use customer, product and interaction data to deliver more relevant recommendations, offers and experiences that keep customers coming back.

### Automate retail operations

Automate repetitive work across customer service, operations and commercial teams, reducing manual effort, speeding up processes and freeing people to focus on higher-value work.

### Create better retail experiences

Build AI into customer and employee experiences to help people find products, get answers, make decisions and get work done faster.


THE SHIFT

## From disconnected tools to connected retail intelligence.

### The problem

- Your teams have data everywhere but still can't get a complete picture.
- You've added AI tools, but the processes haven't really changed.
- You're automating individual tasks, but the wider journey is still manual.
- You're investing in AI, but struggling to link it to revenue, cost savings or customer outcomes.

### The opportunity

- Connect the information needed around the workflow.
- Redesign the workflow around what AI can actually do.
- Connect agents, systems and people across the workflow.
- Build AI around measurable outcomes such as increased revenue, lower costs and better customer experiences.


HOW Fyntix HELPS

## We help retail businesses use AI to increase revenue, improve customer experiences and automate operations.

### AI workflow services

Identify high-value processes, redesign how they should work with AI, and build the agents and integrations that automate work, reduce costs and speed up execution.

### AI product development

Build new AI-powered products and customer experiences, or add AI to existing platforms to increase conversion, improve loyalty and create new revenue streams.

### Customer & commercial intelligence

Connect the data teams need to understand customers, identify opportunities and make better decisions across sales, marketing and operations.


PROCESS

## How we work with retail teams.

### 01 Map the stack

Understand your systems, data and workflows to identify where manual work, lost revenue or poor customer experiences are holding the business back.

### 02 Focus on high value work

Prioritise the workflows where AI can increase revenue, reduce costs, improve service or save significant team time.

### 03 Build and integrate

Create the agents, products and integrations needed to automate the work and put AI into the hands of the people who need it.

### 04 Hand over ownership

Your business owns the capability, so you can keep improving it, scale it and build on it without being locked into another AI tool.
