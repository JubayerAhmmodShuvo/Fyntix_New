## Search & AI Visibility Get found in search and AI answers.

Customers discover businesses through search results, AI answers and the content they trust. A strong presence connects those moments instead of treating each channel as a separate task. We bring SEO, GEO, AEO and AIO into one strategy: understanding your audience, finding the questions that matter, and making your expertise clear across the pages and platforms they use. The goal is relevant discovery that leads to enquiries and sales, supported by a sound technical foundation and content people can use.

Your search presence needs a joined-up plan. We audit what can be crawled and indexed, map the questions your customers ask, and prioritise the pages that deserve attention. Content briefs, internal links and structured data become part of a practical publishing plan. We track relevant visibility, visits, enquiries and conversions, and review AI answer mentions where they can be observed. ## Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about search visibility, paid media, content and working with Fyntix.

What kind of agency is Fyntix?

Fyntix is a growth strategy agency. We connect search and AI visibility, paid advertising and content strategy so your business can be discovered, understood and chosen. Our two service groups are Search & AI Visibility and Paid Media & Content.

Which services does Fyntix offer?

Our services cover SEO strategy and optimisation; GEO, AEO and AIO; AI search visibility; Google Ads; Meta Ads and TikTok Ads; and content strategy and creation, including articles, blogs and social content. We shape the combination around your audience, offer and business goals.

How do SEO, GEO, AEO and AIO fit together?

We treat them as connected parts of a search strategy. SEO focuses on organic search foundations, while answer and generative search work makes your expertise clearer for AI-assisted discovery. Technical access, useful answers, structured information and credible sources support the overall approach.

Can you guarantee rankings or mentions in AI answers?

No. Rankings, ad performance and AI-generated answers are controlled by platforms and can change. We focus on the factors we can work on, agree practical success measures and report what we can observe, without promising a particular ranking, citation or return.

Do you manage Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads?

Yes. We plan and manage campaigns around audience intent, budgets, creative, landing pages and conversion goals. Google Ads, Facebook and Instagram campaigns through Meta Ads, and TikTok Ads can form part of one paid media strategy.

Do you write articles and blogs as well as plan content?

Yes. We support both the strategy and the creation: research, topic selection, content briefs, editorial calendars, articles, blogs, social posts and ad copy. We plan how each piece moves people from discovery to consideration and action.

How will we measure progress?

We agree the measures around your goals, such as qualified enquiries, sales, organic visits and conversions. Paid campaigns can also track acquisition costs and return where reliable attribution is available. AI visibility reviews use a defined set of relevant questions and observed mentions rather than a promise of complete coverage.

Can we start with one service or a growth audit?

Yes. Start with a focused search and AI visibility audit, a paid media review, a content strategy or an agreed combination. We use that starting point to identify priorities, define a practical scope and decide what to work on next.

How do we get started?

Tell us about your business, audience, website, current channels and growth goals through the contact page. We will discuss where search, paid media or content could help and agree a clear scope before the work begins.
