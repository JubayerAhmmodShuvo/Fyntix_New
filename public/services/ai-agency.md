## AI agency FAQs

Straight answers for teams evaluating an AI agency in London.

### What is an AI agency?

An AI agency helps businesses adopt artificial intelligence - typically some mix of automating existing processes, building custom AI agents, and developing AI-native software. Fyntix covers all three under one embedded team: AI automation, agent development, and AI software development.

### What is the difference between an AI agency and an AI consultancy?

An AI consultancy typically advises on strategy and stops at recommendations. An AI agency builds - it ships the automation, agents, or software it recommends. Fyntix does both: AI Launchpad workshops and proofs of concept for strategy, then embedded squads that build and ship.

### What does an AI automation agency actually automate?

The repetitive, rules-based work slowing teams down - content and creative approvals, data entry, reporting, customer operations, and similar workflows. We map the highest-value processes first, then automate with AI rather than rigid scripts, so the automation adapts as the work changes.

### How is Fyntix different from other AI development agencies?

Most AI development agencies specialise in one layer - automation, agents, or software. We combine all three in one embedded squad, backed by delivery experience spanning scale-ups and enterprises: Google, Tesco, Sage, Revolut, and Upstix. Small senior teams, startup pace, production quality.

### Do you work with scale-ups as well as enterprises?

Yes. We work with ambitious scale-ups and global enterprises - from Upstix and Revolut to Google and Tesco. The engagement model adapts to your stage: discovery sprints and proofs of concept for early bets, embedded product squads for full automation, agent, or software builds.

### How much does it cost to work with an AI agency?

It depends on scope and engagement model. We typically start with a fixed-scope AI Launchpad workshop or proof of concept to validate value before a larger build, then move to an embedded squad for delivery. Get in touch for a scoped estimate.

### How do I choose an AI development agency?

Look for shipped evidence, not slide decks - ask what they've automated or built, who will actually work on your project, and how fast they get something real in front of users. Then start small: a Launchpad workshop or proof of concept reveals more than any pitch.

Our HQ is in Southwark, London - The Binary, 27 Copperfield Street, SE1 0EN. We work with clients across the UK and internationally, from scale-ups to global enterprises.
