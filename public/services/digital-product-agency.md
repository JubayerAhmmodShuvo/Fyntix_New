## Digital product agency FAQs

Straight answers for teams evaluating a digital product agency in London.

### What is a digital product agency?

A digital product agency designs, builds, and scales software products - web and mobile apps, platforms, and digital services. Unlike a marketing or branding agency, its core output is working software; unlike a pure development shop, it combines product strategy and design with engineering. The best digital product agencies now build AI-native products by default.

### What does a digital product agency do?

Typical work spans product strategy and discovery, UX and UI design, engineering, and ongoing iteration after launch. At Fyntix that includes rapid proofs of concept, full product builds, and embedding AI capabilities such as agents and automation into existing products.

### How is Fyntix different from other digital product agencies?

We're AI-native. Every product we ship is designed for the era of intelligent software - backed by delivery experience spanning scale-ups and enterprises: Google, Tesco, Sage, Revolut, and Upstix. Small senior squads, startup pace, production quality.

### Can your engineers work inside our team?

Yes. We hire and manage them, and they work embedded with your people.

### What is the difference between a digital product agency and a development shop?

A development shop focuses on writing code to a specification. A digital product agency owns the full product lifecycle - strategy, discovery, UX and UI design, engineering, and iteration after launch. You get senior practitioners who challenge assumptions and ship products people use, not just features on a backlog.

### Do you work with scale-ups as well as enterprises?

Yes. We work with ambitious scale-ups and global enterprises - from Upstix and Revolut to Google and Tesco. The engagement model adapts to your stage: discovery sprints and proofs of concept for early bets, embedded product squads for full builds.

### How much does a digital product agency cost?

It depends on scope and engagement model. We typically start with a fixed-scope discovery or proof of concept to validate value before a larger build, then move to an embedded product squad for delivery. Get in touch for a scoped estimate.

### How do I choose a digital product agency in London?

Look at shipped products rather than showreels: ask who will actually work on your product, how fast they get working software in front of users, and how they handle AI capability. Then start small - a discovery sprint or proof of concept reveals more than any pitch.

### What should I look for in a digital product agency?

Prioritise teams with shipped product evidence, senior practitioners on the work (not a pitch team), a clear discovery or proof-of-concept entry point, and experience embedding AI into products - not just adding a chatbot. Strong digital product agencies combine strategy, UX and UI design, and engineering in one squad.

### Where is your digital product agency in London based?

Our HQ is in Southwark, London - The Binary, 27 Copperfield Street, SE1 0EN. We work with clients across the UK and internationally, from scale-ups to global enterprises.
