## AI agent development FAQs

Straight answers for teams evaluating an AI agent development company.

### What is AI agent development?

AI agent development is the process of designing, building and deploying AI agents - systems that can reason about a task and take action, not just generate a response. That includes defining the agent's business logic, connecting it to real tools and data, and adding the permissions and guardrails needed to run it safely.

### What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent acts - it can read from and write to your systems, chain together multiple steps, and complete a task on your behalf within defined guardrails. Most of what we build sits closer to the agent end: connected to real tools, not just a conversation window.

### Do you offer custom AI solutions or off-the-shelf agents?

Custom. Off-the-shelf AI assistants are built for the general case; we design agents around your specific business logic, data, and workflows, so the agent actually fits how your team works rather than the other way round.

### What does AI integration services work involve?

Connecting an agent to your existing systems - CRM, internal APIs, data warehouse, knowledge base, and tools like Slack - with proper authentication, permissions, and access control, so the agent has real context to act on rather than working from a static prompt.

### Is agentic AI safe to run at enterprise scale?

It can be, with the right guardrails: scoped permissions, business-logic constraints, human-in-the-loop checkpoints where needed, and observability so you can see what an agent did and why. We build these in from day one rather than bolting them on after a proof of concept.

### How is Fyntix different from other AI agent development companies?

We combine agent design, systems integration, and enterprise-grade guardrails in one embedded squad, backed by delivery experience spanning scale-ups and enterprises: Google, Tesco, Sage, Revolut, and YourAvatar. Small senior teams, startup pace, production quality.

### Do you work with scale-ups as well as enterprises?

Yes. We work with ambitious scale-ups and global enterprises - from YourAvatar and Upstix to Google and Tesco. The engagement model adapts to your stage: discovery sprints and proofs of concept for early bets, embedded squads for full agent builds and integration.

### Can your engineers work inside our team?

Yes. We hire and manage them, and they work embedded with your people.

Our HQ is in Southwark, London - The Binary, 27 Copperfield Street, SE1 0EN. We work with clients across the UK and internationally, from scale-ups to global enterprises.
