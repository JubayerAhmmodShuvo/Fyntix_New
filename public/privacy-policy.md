# Privacy Policy

Updated 24 November 2026

Fyntix is a business growth agency offering Search & AI Visibility and Paid Media & Content. This policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit this website or contact us about SEO, GEO, AEO, AIO, AI search visibility, paid advertising, content or growth strategy.

## Who is responsible

Fyntix is responsible for personal information it receives directly. For privacy questions, access requests or complaints, email [info@fyntix.com](mailto:info@fyntix.com), with “Privacy” in the subject, or write to Fyntix, 248 Coronation Dr, Scarborough, ON M1E 2J4, Canada.

## Information you choose to share

If you contact us by email or post, we receive the contact details, business information, message and any attachments you send. If you enquire about joining the team, this can include your work history, portfolio and application details. Please share only information relevant to your enquiry and avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information.

The website’s enquiry and application forms are not currently connected to a submission service. Entering information or selecting an attachment in these forms does not send it to Fyntix or upload it to our server. To send an enquiry or application, email [info@fyntix.com](mailto:info@fyntix.com). Your browser may independently offer autofill; that feature is controlled by your browser.

## Information involved in visiting the site

Your browser sends technical information to the infrastructure that delivers this Vercel-hosted website. This can include an IP address, requested URL, request time and browser or device details needed to deliver pages and support security. Hosting providers may process operational and security logs under their own terms. Fyntix has not installed visitor analytics or advertising tags on this website.

We store a first-party preference cookie to remember your cookie choice. Optional Vimeo players load only after you allow external media. Loading a player shares technical information with Vimeo, which may use its own cookies or storage. The [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy) explains these choices and how to withdraw permission.

## Why we use information

- To respond to enquiries and discuss whether our growth services are a suitable fit.
- To prepare proposals, manage agreed work and communicate with clients or suppliers.
- To consider applications and respond to people interested in joining Fyntix.
- To remember privacy preferences, deliver the site and address security issues.
- To maintain records needed for contractual, accounting or legal obligations.

We do not sell website visitors’ personal information. An enquiry is not permission to add you to an unrelated marketing list. Any separate marketing communications must respect the permissions and unsubscribe choices that apply to them.

## Consent and your choices

We explain the purpose for collecting information and seek consent where required. You can decline optional external media without losing access to the rest of this website. You can also ask us to stop an optional use of information by emailing [info@fyntix.com](mailto:info@fyntix.com). Withdrawing consent does not undo processing already completed and may affect a service that depends on the information. We will explain any applicable limits.

## Who may receive information

Information may be handled by people at Fyntix and providers needed for hosting, email, security and agreed business administration. If you enable a Vimeo player, Vimeo receives the information needed to provide that external service. Other sites you choose to open, such as map or social links, handle your information under their own policies.

For client work, access to advertising accounts, search data, lead information and reporting tools is governed by the agreed scope and any applicable data-processing terms. This website policy does not replace a client’s own privacy notice or authorise new tracking on a client’s website.

Providers may process information outside Canada. Information processed in another country can be subject to that country’s laws and lawful access requirements. Contact us if you need information about the providers used for your particular enquiry or engagement.

## Retention and protection

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed for its stated purpose, an ongoing engagement, a dispute or applicable record-keeping duties, then deleted or de-identified as appropriate. The website preference cookie expires after 180 days unless you renew your choice. Provider-controlled log and media-storage periods are determined by the relevant provider.

Access should be limited to people who need the information for the relevant task, with safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please report a suspected privacy or security issue to [info@fyntix.com](mailto:info@fyntix.com).

## Access, correction and concerns

You can ask whether we hold personal information about you, request access or correction, ask about its use or disclosure, or raise a privacy concern. We may need proportionate information to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. Do not send identity documents unless we explain why they are needed and how to provide them appropriately.

We will handle requests within the time and conditions required by applicable law and explain any lawful reason information cannot be provided or removed. You may also contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/report-a-concern/) if you have an unresolved concern.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change. A material new optional use of personal information will be explained and, where required, permission sought before it starts. The date at the top identifies the current version.
