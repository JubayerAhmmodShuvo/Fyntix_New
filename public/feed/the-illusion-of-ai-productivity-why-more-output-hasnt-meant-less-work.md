# More content is useful only when it has a job

Content strategy · Fyntix Editorial · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Give every article, social post and advertisement a defined reader, useful question and natural next step before increasing production.

It is possible to publish regularly while leaving important customer questions unanswered. Production becomes easier to measure than usefulness, so teams can find themselves optimising the calendar instead of the role the material plays in a decision.

Before commissioning another piece, define its job. Identify the reader, the question and the information that would help. Then decide how the piece connects with a relevant service, campaign or further explanation.

## Write the purpose into the brief

A useful brief should describe the customer's situation and the reason this piece deserves attention. Include the expertise or evidence the writer needs and the decision the reader should be better equipped to make afterwards.

For an illustrative service article, the purpose might be to explain the information required for an accurate quote. That gives the writer a practical outcome and helps the business review the draft for completeness.

## Choose the format around the task

A short social post can introduce an idea or answer a narrow question. A guide can explore a considered decision. An advertisement needs a clear offer and an appropriate destination. Give each format an achievable role rather than expecting every asset to explain the whole business.

Repurposing can be useful when the substance fits the new context. Adapt the explanation and next step instead of copying the same block of text into every channel.

## Add information the business can stand behind

Specific examples, process detail and useful comparisons make content more valuable to a reader. Ask the relevant specialist to confirm claims and explain important limits. Editing should improve clarity while retaining the meaning of the source information.

Google's guidance on generative AI content warns about producing pages at scale without adding value for users. Whatever tools support production, the business still needs an editorial standard and a reason for each page to exist.

## Review contribution rather than volume alone

Look at the questions bringing readers to the piece, the information they use next and the feedback staff receive. A practical guide may help a sales conversation even when the original visit does not immediately create an enquiry. Choose measures that fit the intended job.
