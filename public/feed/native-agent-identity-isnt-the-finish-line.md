# Make your business information consistent for AI discovery

AI search visibility · Fyntix Editorial · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Give customers and discovery systems a consistent account of your services, contact details, markets and supporting evidence.

A business can be described differently across its website, profiles, directory listings and older articles. Customers may encounter any of those descriptions while researching a service. Inconsistency creates uncertainty about what the business does and which information is current.

Begin with the information your own team can confirm. A clear, maintained source makes it easier to improve pages and identify outdated descriptions elsewhere. The task is information quality, with a practical role in both human and AI-assisted discovery.

## Create an approved business description

Describe the company, its services, intended customers and actual markets. Include contact details and the next step for someone who wants help. Avoid broad claims that the business cannot explain or support.

For example, a company offering local services should distinguish its office location from its service coverage. Those details answer different questions and should remain accurate wherever they appear.

## Review the important public surfaces

Compare the main website pages with the profiles and listings customers are likely to use. Identify outdated offers, inconsistent names and contact routes that no longer work. Prioritise sources the business controls or can reasonably request to update.

Record the owner and review date for each important surface. Consistency becomes easier to maintain when updates have a process, rather than relying on someone remembering every place the company is mentioned.

## Keep evidence attached to its context

Case studies, examples and specialist explanations should preserve their meaning when reused. A result needs its measure and reporting period. A service claim needs enough detail to help a reader assess fit. Avoid turning qualified information into a universal promise.

This matters when preparing concise summaries for different channels. Shorter wording should still reflect the underlying service accurately, with a route to fuller information for people who need it.

## Observe representation without overclaiming control

Review relevant customer questions and record how the business is described in the experiences you are investigating. Note inaccurate information and the sources that can be inspected. Keep mentions and citations separate from visits or enquiries in the report.
