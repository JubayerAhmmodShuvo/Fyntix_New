# Search and AI discovery: build a useful presence in both

AI search visibility · Fyntix Editorial · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Connect established search work with clear, useful answers while keeping observations of AI discovery separate from business outcomes.

Customers can encounter a business through a search result, a generated answer or information shared elsewhere. The practical challenge is to make the business's services and expertise understandable across those moments without losing sight of the customer journey.

Begin with useful information on your own website. Clear service pages, relevant explanations and contextual evidence support people evaluating the business. They also give a search-and-answer visibility programme a dependable source to review and improve.

## Connect the work through customer questions

Map questions about the problem, the available approaches and the suitability of your service. Identify which pages currently answer them and where useful information is missing. Keep each proposed improvement connected to an actual decision.

For an illustrative business service, a prospect may need to understand the delivery process before comparing providers. A precise explanation can help across several discovery routes, rather than being written only for one interface.

## Keep the search foundations in view

Review technical access, page purpose and the links connecting related information. Google states that established SEO practices remain relevant to its AI search features and that no additional special optimisation is required for inclusion. That guidance supports a focus on useful fundamentals rather than an unexplained technical shortcut.

Other platforms have their own guidance and behaviours. Ask the specialist to identify the relevant source for a platform-specific recommendation and make clear which parts of a plan are being investigated experimentally.

## Preserve the meaning of the evidence

A case-study result should include its measure and context. A service explanation should identify scope and fit. Content becomes less useful when a specific result is reused as a general promise or when the reader cannot tell which claim the evidence supports.

Review the information with the people responsible for the service. The publishing process needs both clear expression and dependable substance.

## Measure discovery and subsequent action separately

Keep sampled mentions and citations distinct from referral visits, enquiries and customers. Record the conditions of an AI-answer review so later observations have context. Use the business's own website and sales information to understand the actions that follow.

Google's [guidance on AI features and websites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) is a useful reference when assessing proposed search work.
