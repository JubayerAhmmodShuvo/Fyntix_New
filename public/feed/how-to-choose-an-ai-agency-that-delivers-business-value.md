# How to choose an agency around business outcomes

Growth strategy · Fyntix Editorial · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Compare agency proposals through the customer outcome, implementation plan and evidence that will guide the working relationship.

Two agency proposals can list the same channels while describing very different working relationships. One may promise a quantity of activity; another may explain the customer problem, the delivery process and the decisions that activity supports. Compare the relationship between those elements.

Start with an outcome the business can define. A qualified consultation, a suitable quote request or a completed purchase provides a clearer reference point than a broad ambition to improve online performance.

## Check how the agency interprets your problem

Ask the team to explain the gap it sees and the evidence it would review. A useful answer should connect the offer, audience and customer journey. It should also identify what remains unknown rather than assuming the solution was settled before the conversation.

For example, a business seeking better enquiries might need clearer service information, stronger discovery or a more dependable follow-up process. The proposal should explain which possibility deserves investigation first.

## Connect deliverables with their purpose

Request an explanation of what each proposed workstream is intended to change. Articles should answer relevant questions. Search improvements should support useful pages. Paid campaigns should have an appropriate audience, message and destination.

This does not make output unimportant. It gives output a reason to exist. A commitment to publish material is easier to assess when the business understands which decisions the material should help customers make.

## Review the implementation arrangement

Clarify who approves information, makes website changes and supplies customer feedback. Ask how dependencies are recorded and how the agency handles an item that cannot move. A strong strategy still needs a workable delivery process.

Look at a sample report and ask what decision it would support. Keep reporting windows, conversion definitions and the source of any customer-outcome information visible. A confident headline should not replace the explanation behind it.

## Choose a partner you can question

Useful collaboration includes disagreement and revision. Ask what evidence would cause the agency to change its plan. Notice whether the team can explain trade-offs plainly and connect them with the business's priorities.
