# GEO, AEO and AIO: what should your business work on?

AI search visibility · Fyntix Editorial · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Translate SEO, GEO, AEO and AIO into concrete research, content and measurement work that supports relevant customer discovery.

Search terminology can expand faster than a business's understanding of the work being proposed. SEO, GEO, AEO and AIO are used in overlapping ways across the industry. A useful brief should explain the concrete tasks behind the labels and the customer problem they support.

At Fyntix, we discuss these disciplines within Search & AI Visibility. The starting point is how people discover, understand and choose the business, with a practical plan for improving the information available to them.

## Keep the underlying work visible

SEO work can include technical access, intent research, page relevance and internal structure. Answer-led and generative-discovery work can involve clearer explanations, dependable business information and reviews of how relevant questions are answered.

Ask the provider to identify which pages, source materials and observations the programme will involve. The labels should make the scope easier to discuss, rather than substitute for an explanation of what will be done.

## Begin with a useful customer question

Choose an important decision and identify the information a person needs. Review whether your website answers it well and whether the answer connects naturally with the service. This gives both content and technical work a purpose.

For an illustrative professional service, the question may concern the type of help available for a specific situation. A useful page explains scope and process clearly, then gives the reader an appropriate route to discuss their requirements.

## Ask for platform-specific evidence

When a recommendation concerns a particular search or AI experience, ask which guidance supports it. Distinguish established requirements from a working hypothesis or experiment. A technical addition should have a clear purpose before it becomes part of the plan.

The business should also confirm the substance of published claims. Structure and clarity help readers, but they do not make inaccurate information dependable.

## Agree a report the business can understand

Record relevant visibility observations, visits and enquiries as distinct measures. Explain the conditions of any sampled AI-answer review. Connect the next tasks with gaps identified through the research and customer feedback.

Fyntix uses the service terminology to organise related work, with practical deliverables around research, content structure, technical foundations and reporting. When evaluating a proposal, ask the team to describe the customer question, the planned improvement and the evidence it will review. Those details give the business a clearer basis for action than the acronyms alone.
