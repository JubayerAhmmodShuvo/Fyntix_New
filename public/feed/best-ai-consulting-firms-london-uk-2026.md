# What to include in a search and AI visibility brief

AI search visibility · Fyntix Editorial · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Brief AI visibility work with a defined audience, useful customer questions, accurate business information and realistic observation methods.

An AI visibility brief should explain what customers need to understand about your business. Beginning with a demand to appear in every answer produces a vague objective that is difficult to assess. Begin instead with the audience, the offer and the questions that influence a decision.

Include your existing search presence in the discussion. SEO, content clarity and AI-assisted discovery overlap in practical ways, but different platforms can present information differently. The brief should give the team a useful investigation, rather than assuming one universal ranking measure.

## Define a set of customer questions

Collect questions from sales conversations, support enquiries and the content people already use. Separate questions about your own business from broader comparisons and buying criteria. Give each question a reason for inclusion.

For example, a software business might investigate how buyers compare solutions for a specific workflow. The brief should explain the workflow, the intended customer and the evidence the business can provide about its capabilities.

## Provide accurate source material

Share the main service pages, product information, relevant case studies and approved business descriptions. Identify material that needs updating. Useful work depends on knowing which statements are accurate and which claims require further evidence.

Name the people who can review specialist explanations. The agency can improve structure and clarity, but the business should confirm the substance of its expertise. This matters when an answer could otherwise oversimplify a service or present an unsupported claim.

## Agree what will be observed

Specify the questions, platforms and review conditions. Record whether an observation concerns a mention, a citation, a referral visit or an enquiry. These describe different interactions and should remain distinct in the report.

Answers can vary between sessions and contexts. Treat observations as a defined sample, then use website and customer information to understand subsequent behaviour. A single answer is a starting point for investigation rather than proof of dependable demand.

## Ask for a prioritised improvement plan

The output should explain which pages, answers or business details need attention and why. Connect proposed changes with the questions in the brief. Agree who will implement and review them before commissioning a large content programme.
