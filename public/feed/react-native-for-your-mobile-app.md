# Make mobile landing pages work for paid traffic

Google Ads · Fyntix Editorial · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Review the message, reading experience and enquiry process on a phone before expanding paid traffic to a landing page.

A landing page can look convincing on a large screen while becoming difficult to use on a phone. Important information may appear too late, controls can feel cramped and the enquiry form may require more effort than the visitor expects.

Review the mobile journey as a customer task. Open the advertisement's destination, understand the offer and complete the intended action. That exercise often reveals more useful priorities than judging the page from a static desktop screenshot.

## Confirm the offer quickly

The opening should explain what the visitor has arrived to assess. Keep the main service and its relevant qualification details easy to find. A large decorative introduction should not make the visitor search for the promise in the advertisement.

For an illustrative local campaign, service coverage may matter immediately. If the business can only help customers in a defined area, make that clear before asking the visitor to work through a long page or form.

## Make the information comfortable to read

Review text size, contrast, paragraph length and the spacing between controls. Check that headings help a reader scan the page. Images should support understanding without making the useful information difficult to reach.

Test the experience on the devices and connections relevant to the audience. If the page is slow or elements shift while it loads, involve the person responsible for the website and prioritise the issues affecting the main customer task.

## Reduce unnecessary form work

Ask for the information needed to respond usefully. Explain unfamiliar fields and provide clear feedback when something needs correcting. Confirm what happens after a successful submission so the visitor does not have to guess whether the enquiry arrived.

Keep the business process in mind. Removing every qualification question can increase submissions while making follow-up less useful. The aim is a sensible balance between customer effort and the information needed to help.

## Review mobile responses separately

Where the available data supports it, examine the mobile journey alongside other devices. Combine completion information with feedback about the enquiries received. A higher conversion rate is only part of the picture if customer fit changes at the same time.
