# Client Data & Account Access

Fyntix’s Search & AI Visibility and Paid Media & Content work can involve business accounts, search data, campaign reporting, content and lead information. This page explains what should be agreed before that access is provided. It is an overview, not a signed data-processing agreement.

## Agree the purpose and scope first

The project scope should identify the services, tools, data categories and authorised contacts involved. Access should be limited to information needed for the agreed SEO, AI visibility, advertising, content or reporting tasks. Do not provide customer databases or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.

## Use appropriate account permissions

Where possible, use platform roles or delegated access for services such as search reporting and advertising accounts. Share only the level of access needed for the agreed work. Avoid sending passwords by email and review or remove access when a project ends.

## Document responsibilities for personal information

If Fyntix will process personal information on a client’s behalf, the parties should agree any required processing terms before that processing begins. Those terms should address instructions, purposes, access, confidentiality, security measures, providers and processing locations, assistance with requests or incidents, retention and return or deletion when the work ends.

The client’s own privacy notices, consent mechanisms and platform configurations must reflect the tracking and data uses actually approved for its campaigns. Fyntix’s website cookie choice does not provide consent for tracking visitors on a client’s website.

## Request the right documents

For a project-specific discussion, email [info@fyntix.com](mailto:info@fyntix.com). The [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) covers this website and direct enquiries, while the applicable written service and data-processing agreements govern an agreed client engagement.
