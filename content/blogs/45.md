# Plan for the questions customers ask in ChatGPT

An AI discovery review should begin with the decisions customers make, rather than a long list of prompts designed to produce a favourable screenshot. Useful questions come from the audience's situation and the information it needs to evaluate an option.

Collect those questions from the people who speak with prospects. Include the context that changes the answer, such as location, service scope or intended use. That makes the review more relevant to the business and easier to repeat.

## Separate question types

Distinguish questions about your own company from broader explanations and provider comparisons. A customer checking a service detail needs a different answer from someone trying to understand a category for the first time.

For an illustrative software company, a question about suitability for a particular workflow may be more informative than a broad request for the best software. The relevant context helps the team identify which parts of the offer need clearer explanation.

## Record the review conditions

Define the questions and the experience being observed. Keep a record of the date and relevant context, and describe whether the answer mentions the business or cites a source. Use the same framework when reviewing later observations.

Answers can differ between sessions and conditions. A sample helps identify patterns and gaps, but it should not be presented as a complete measure of how every customer discovers the business.

## Inspect the information behind the answer

Review the sources that can be inspected and compare them with approved business information. Look for outdated descriptions, missing service details or questions the website does not answer well. Prioritise changes the business can substantiate and maintain.

The useful improvement may be a clearer service explanation, a contextual comparison or a more precise description of customer fit. Give each proposed piece a reader and a purpose rather than producing content solely around a prompt phrase.

## Connect the review with the wider journey

Keep observed answers separate from referral visits and enquiries. Where reliable referral information exists, review what those visitors do next. Ask the sales team whether prospects mention AI tools during their research, while treating that feedback as another source of context.
