# How to assess a technical SEO audit

A technical SEO audit should help a team understand which website issues deserve attention and how to address them. The number of warnings in an export is not a measure of its usefulness. What matters is the interpretation and the route from observation to implementation.

Review the audit with the people who maintain the website. Their context can reveal deliberate choices, platform constraints and dependencies that an external scan cannot establish on its own.

## Ask for evidence at the page level

An important finding should identify affected pages and explain what was observed. Ask for examples that a developer or content owner can inspect. Broad statements about website health need enough detail to support a specific action.

For example, several URLs with similar titles may indicate a content problem, a technical convention or pages serving genuinely different purposes. The audit should investigate that context before recommending a blanket fix.

## Connect the issue with a consequence

Ask how the finding affects discovery, interpretation or the visitor's experience. A clear explanation makes priorities easier to assess alongside other business work. It also helps distinguish an urgent problem on a core service page from a minor inconsistency elsewhere.

Avoid treating every tool label as a universal judgement. The specialist should explain why the observation matters for this website and where further investigation is needed.

## Review the proposed implementation

Each priority recommendation should describe the intended change, dependencies and owner. Clarify whether the work belongs with development, content or another part of the business. If several options exist, ask for the trade-off behind the preferred approach.

Include a check after release. Confirm that the live site reflects the intended change and that related functionality remains usable. Keep a record of affected pages and implementation dates so later reviews have context.

## Expect a focused work queue

The audit should lead to a manageable sequence of tasks. Review that sequence as business priorities and evidence change. Completion of a technical fix is an observed delivery result; its effect on search activity needs a suitable review period and careful interpretation.
