# What a paid media agency should report every month

A monthly report should help a business decide what to keep, change or investigate. Platform exports contain useful information, but presenting every available number can obscure the customer journey. Start with the goal of the campaign and organise the report around it.

Keep the reporting period and comparison visible. Explain any material changes in spending, campaign scope or event definitions before interpreting movement. Context turns a collection of metrics into a review the business can use.

## Show where the budget went

Summarise spending by the distinctions that matter to the plan, such as channel, service or market. Connect the allocation with the question being tested. If part of the budget explored a new audience, say so rather than judging it as though it served the same purpose as an established campaign.

Include meaningful changes made during the period. A new offer, landing page or targeting decision can affect how the result should be read. Keep the explanation concise enough to remain useful in a review meeting.

## Define the response being counted

State what a conversion means in this report. Separate enquiries, bookings and purchases where they represent different actions. Show the cost associated with the relevant response and make clear when the underlying volume is limited.

For lead generation, include sales feedback when available. A low cost per submitted form can look attractive while producing unsuitable requests. The business needs to understand the quality of the response as well as its quantity.

## Explain channel comparisons carefully

Advertising platforms can use different reporting and attribution settings. Record those settings before comparing reported conversions. Review the business's own records alongside platform data, and investigate overlaps rather than adding every reported result together.

A useful report acknowledges when a question remains unresolved. If the information cannot yet distinguish new demand from existing interest, describe the next investigation instead of presenting a stronger conclusion than the evidence supports.

## Finish with a decision list

Summarise the main finding, the proposed action, its owner and the next review point. A report that produces a clear work queue is more useful than a deck that repeats the same observations each month.
