# Protect the quality of your marketing data

A campaign report is only as useful as the meaning behind its numbers. A conversion may refer to a submitted form, a clicked phone link or a completed purchase. If those actions are mixed together, the total can rise without telling the business whether it is gaining suitable customers.

Begin with a short measurement specification. Describe the action, when it should be counted and where the team will review it. Include the person responsible for checking that the definition still matches the customer journey.

## Define an event in business language

Write the meaning before discussing the tracking tool. For an enquiry form, decide whether the event represents a successful submission or an attempted submission. For a booking, distinguish selecting a date from receiving confirmation. Those steps can help explain behaviour, but they should remain separate measures.

Ask the team to walk through a real example. If two people describe the same conversion differently, resolve that disagreement before using the event to evaluate performance.

## Check the complete journey

Test the relevant action on the devices and routes customers use. Confirm that a successful interaction is recorded as intended and that errors do not produce the same success signal. Repeat the check when forms, booking tools or landing pages change.

Look for duplicate counting across integrations. A useful measurement review should establish whether two records describe the same action, rather than assuming every recorded event represents another customer. Keep a brief record of what was checked and what remains uncertain.

## Keep collection proportionate

Collect the information needed to understand the enquiry and follow it up. Review consent requirements and platform rules with the people responsible for them before changing a tracking setup. Data quality improves when the business understands what it collects and why.

Give staff access appropriate to their role and keep the account owner clear. This is a practical operating responsibility, especially when several agencies or team members work on the same marketing programme.

## Explain changes in the report

If an event definition changes, mark the date and explain the effect on comparisons. A measurement correction can create a visible jump that should not be presented as campaign growth. Where reliable history is unavailable, establish a new baseline and label it clearly.
