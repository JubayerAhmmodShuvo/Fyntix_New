# What to ask before hiring a Meta Ads agency

A Meta Ads proposal should explain how the team will help an appropriate audience understand and respond to your offer. Platform knowledge matters, but the campaign also needs useful creative, a suitable destination and a response the business can handle.

Begin by describing the customer decision. A familiar product, a specialist service and a considered consultation may need different messages and evidence. Ask the agency to explain that context before discussing a fixed campaign package.

## How will you develop creative ideas?

Ask where the team will find the questions, objections and use cases that inform the advertisements. Look for a process that includes customer knowledge and approved evidence. The creative plan should identify meaningful ideas to test, rather than rely only on cosmetic variations.

For example, a service business might compare a demonstration of its process with an explanation of a common customer concern. Those ideas give the team something useful to learn about the audience's response.

## Who owns the experience after the click?

Clarify who reviews the landing page, form and confirmation. The page should continue the offer presented in the advertisement. If the business needs a consultation before providing a price, make that process clear rather than suggesting an immediate transaction.

Ask how the agency will use enquiry feedback. A campaign can attract attention while leaving the business with unsuitable requests. The message and qualification information deserve review alongside platform activity.

## What will the report establish?

Confirm the conversion definitions and attribution context used in reporting. Discuss how platform-reported actions will be reviewed alongside website and business records. Distinguish a reported response from evidence of an additional customer caused by the campaign.

Ask for a sample report that ends with decisions. It should explain spending, useful responses and what the team intends to test or improve next, with enough context to understand the comparison.

## What does the collaboration require?

Agree access, creative approvals, factual review and the responsibilities of both teams. Confirm what is included in the management scope and where additional production or website work would be needed.

Fyntix connects Meta campaigns with paid media strategy, content and the customer journey. Before hiring a partner, look for a practical explanation of how the offer becomes creative, how the response is assessed and how that learning changes the next round of work.
