# Google Ads, Meta or TikTok: choose a useful first test

The right first paid channel depends on the customer journey and the question the business wants to investigate. Starting with a platform because it is familiar can lead to a campaign that does not match the offer or the resources available to support it.

Define the audience, the response you need and the information a prospect requires before acting. Then assess the channel in that context. The initial decision should create a useful test, rather than settle the entire future media plan.

## Consider the customer's starting point

Search campaigns can engage people expressing a question or need through a query. Social campaigns can present an offer while people are doing something else. In either case, attention does not automatically establish readiness to buy.

For an illustrative urgent local service, a specific search may be an important entry point. A less familiar product may need more explanation and demonstration. Investigate the behaviour relevant to the offer instead of assigning every business in a category the same channel.

## Match the creative requirement

Review the material the business can produce and maintain. A useful social programme needs creative ideas that suit the audience and format. A search programme needs relevant messages and destinations that answer the intent behind the query.

Do not treat the website as a separate concern. Each campaign should lead to information that carries through the offer and makes the intended response clear. Include the work required to improve that journey in the plan.

## Choose a limited question

The first test might explore a market, message or customer use case. Keep the scope manageable and record the assumptions. A limited budget can become hard to interpret when spread across several unrelated offers and platforms at once.

Agree how results will be reviewed. TikTok's attribution guidance, for example, defines platform-reported actions within particular reporting conditions. Check the relevant settings and meanings rather than assuming every platform reports conversions on the same basis.

## Use the result to choose the next step

Review customer fit alongside volume and cost. The useful decision may be to improve the message, refine the destination or test another audience. Early activity can guide learning without establishing a reliable long-term forecast.
