# How Fyntix connects search, paid media and content

Fyntix is a business growth agency. We help businesses become easier to discover, understand and choose through two connected service groups: Search & AI Visibility and Paid Media & Content. The combination begins with the audience and offer, rather than a fixed list of channels.

A useful programme gives each discipline a clear job. Search work supports relevant discovery. Content helps people evaluate a question or service. Paid campaigns bring a defined message to an appropriate audience. Reporting helps the business decide what to improve next.

## Start with the business and customer journey

We begin by understanding what the business offers, who it can help and how a prospect becomes a customer. Existing pages, campaign information and sales feedback provide context for the initial priorities. We also identify the practical constraints that affect delivery.

For example, a business may need a clearer explanation of a specialist service before it benefits from more traffic. Another may have useful content but weak local discovery. The starting investigation should reflect the actual gap.

## Build Search & AI Visibility around useful information

Our search service includes SEO strategy and optimisation, GEO, AEO, AIO and AI search visibility. Work can involve technical foundations, intent research, page structure, internal links and content that explains the business's expertise.

The plan connects those tasks with the questions customers ask. We distinguish observations of visibility from website visits and enquiries, so the reporting retains a clear meaning as discovery experiences change.

## Connect Paid Media & Content with the same offer

Our paid media work can include Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads. We consider audience intent, creative messages, the destination page and the action the business wants a suitable prospect to take. Content strategy and creation support that journey through articles, blogs, ad copy and social content.

These responsibilities should reinforce one another. A campaign's promise needs a useful explanation after the click, while an article should give an interested reader a relevant way to continue.

## Use reporting to maintain the connection

We agree the measures around the business's goals and the information available. Reviews should explain what changed, what was learned and which action follows. Enquiry quality and sales feedback help keep the programme connected to the customer experience.

The scope can be focused or span several disciplines. In either case, the purpose is a coherent growth plan with understandable priorities, clear ownership and a practical path from being found to having a useful customer conversation.
