# How to review a Google Ads management proposal

A Google Ads proposal should explain how campaign management will support the business's offer and sales process. A list of account tasks is useful, but it does not establish whether the team understands the customer response you need.

Use the proposal to review the whole path from a search to an enquiry or purchase. Ask who owns each part, what information is required and how the agency will decide whether the work is helping.

## Clarify the audience and campaign purpose

The proposal should identify the service, market and search intent it plans to investigate. Ask how the team will distinguish relevant demand from loosely related searches. Check whether brand, new-customer discovery and other purposes are being discussed clearly.

For an illustrative specialist service, broad industry interest may be less useful than a narrower group of people looking for a specific solution. The account structure should reflect the business decision rather than simply reproduce a keyword list.

## Ask what counts as a conversion

Confirm whether the programme measures submissions, calls, bookings, purchases or another defined action. Ask how those events will be checked and how sales feedback can inform the review. A form total alone may not show whether the campaign attracts suitable customers.

Google Ads distinguishes qualified leads and converted leads in its conversion-goal guidance. Where appropriate, discuss the information and process needed to connect later lead stages with the campaign. The business needs a workable feedback route for that information to remain useful.

## Include the destination and the message

Ask who reviews landing-page content, form usability and the relationship between the advertisement and the page. Clarify whether proposed improvements are included, separately scoped or expected from your internal team.

The campaign can expose questions the page does not answer. A useful management arrangement gives those observations a route into content and website improvements rather than limiting the discussion to account settings.

## Review access, reporting and change control

Confirm account ownership, the working team's access and the handover process. Ask for a sample report showing spending, useful responses and next actions. Understand which changes can happen within the agreed plan and which require approval.

Fyntix's Google Ads service connects intent, campaigns, creative and conversion reporting. A good proposal should make that connection understandable and identify the responsibilities needed on both sides. You can read Google's own explanation of [qualified and converted leads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11459091?hl=en) when preparing the measurement discussion.
